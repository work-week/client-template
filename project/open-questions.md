# Open questions

Questions for the client or the team. Remove a question once answered and record the answer where it belongs.

## About the business

- Who are their best customers, and who do they want more or fewer of?
- Who do they lose business to, and why?

## Brand and content

- What do they like and dislike in how they look today? Any examples they admire?
- What brand assets exist (logo files, colors, fonts, photos)?

## Access

- Which accounts and properties exist, and who owns each? (Fill in [map/accounts.md](../map/accounts.md).)
